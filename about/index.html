Who We Are

Nigeria's Trusted Solar Energy Company

Peak Renewable is a premier solar energy company based in Lagos, Nigeria, specialising in high-quality solar energy solutions for homes and businesses. We are dedicated to providing reliable, efficient and cost-effective solar installations, maintenance services and expert energy consultation.

Our goal is to empower Nigerian communities with energy independence — freeing families and businesses from the burden of NEPA's unreliable supply and the rising cost of diesel generators. We serve Lagos, Ogun and Ibadan with the same standard of professionalism and genuine care.

Our Core Values

What We Stand For

Integrity

We give honest advice, transparent pricing and realistic expectations — always. No hidden costs. No empty promises. What we say is what we deliver.

Excellence

We use only quality components, employ skilled technicians and apply rigorous quality control. Every installation must perform perfectly — not just on day one but for 25 years.

Community

We are not just a company — we are part of the communities we serve. Every project we complete improves lives, reduces pollution and builds a stronger, more energy-independent Nigeria.

Reliability

We show up. We finish what we start. And we remain available long after installation day. Our maintenance team is always reachable across Lagos, Ogun and Ibadan.

Education

We believe an informed customer makes better decisions. We explain everything clearly — from system sizing to component selection — so you always know exactly what you are getting and why.

Sustainability

Every solar system we install displaces diesel and reduces carbon emissions. Our work is our contribution to a cleaner, healthier planet — starting with Nigeria.
